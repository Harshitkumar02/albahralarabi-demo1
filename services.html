Core Capabilities

Priority Services in Detail

01 — EPC Contracting

Single-Point Responsibility from Design to Commissioning

As a premier EPC contractor, we manage the full project lifecycle, reducing interfaces and delivering predictable schedules, cost and quality for energy and process clients.

  • Detailed engineering and design management
  • Global procurement and logistics coordination
  • Construction, commissioning and handover
02 — Tanks & Terminals

Storage Infrastructure Built to International Standards

We design, fabricate and erect storage tanks, tank farms and terminal facilities for oil, gas and petrochemical clients, in compliance with API and ASME codes.

  • Atmospheric and pressure storage tanks
  • Tank farm piping, pumping and loading systems
  • Terminal and refinery plant facilities
03 — Piping & Pipelines

Precision Piping Systems for Critical Operations

Our certified welders and fabrication teams deliver process piping and pipeline systems with rigorous inspection and testing at every stage.

  • Shop and field piping fabrication
  • Pipeline installation, tie-ins and hydrotesting
  • Welding procedure qualification and NDT
04 — Structural Steel Fabrication

In-House Fabrication with Schedule Certainty

Our 20,000 sqm facility in Hamriyah Free Zone enables full control of quality and delivery for steel structures, skids, modules and equipment.

  • Steel structures, pipe racks and platforms
  • Pressure vessels, skids and modular units
  • Blasting, painting and surface protection
05 — Maintenance & Shutdown

Keeping Your Facilities Operating Safely

We support operators with planned maintenance, turnarounds and shutdown execution delivered by experienced supervisors and a safety-first workforce.

  • Turnaround and shutdown planning and execution
  • Mechanical, piping and electrical maintenance
  • Repair, modification and refurbishment works
