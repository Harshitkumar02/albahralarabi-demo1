About Al Bahr Al Arabi

Company Profile

Engineering Excellence Since 1998

Al Bahr Al Arabi Group is a premier Engineering, Procurement and Construction contractor with over 25 years of distinguished service in the Oil & Gas sector, serving Petrochemical, Power and Desalination clients.

Operating from our 20,000 sqm fabrication facility in Hamriyah Free Zone, Sharjah, we deliver turnkey solutions backed by international standards, a strong safety culture and a highly skilled workforce.
