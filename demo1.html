About Al Bahr Al Arabi Group

Leading EPC Contractor in UAE

Al Bahr Al Arabi Group stands as a premier Engineering, Procurement, and Construction contractor with over 25 years of distinguished service in the Oil & Gas sector. Operating from our state-of-the-art 20,000 sqm fabrication facility in Hamriyah Free Zone, Sharjah, we deliver comprehensive solutions across the UAE and MENA region.

Our commitment to safety, quality, and adherence to international standards including API and ASME certifications ensures that every project meets the highest industry benchmarks. We specialize in tank farms, refinery plants, civil works, and steel fabrication, providing end-to-end solutions that drive operational excellence.
